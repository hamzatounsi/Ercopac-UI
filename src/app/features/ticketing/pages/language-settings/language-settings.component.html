Translation

Per ticket
How it works
  • Each ticket conversation shows your language and the customer's language.
  • Turn on “Translate” on a ticket: the customer's messages appear in your language, with the original below.
  • Your public replies are sent to the customer in their language. Internal notes are never translated.
  • The customer's language comes from their contact record (Settings › Customers) or their own setting.
How it works
  • Write to the support team in your own language.
  • Agents can read your messages translated, and their replies can be translated into your language.

Translation runs on the organisation's own AI service; if it is unavailable, messages are shown untranslated.
